Chocolate Stunts

The classic driving game, Brøderbund Stunts 1.1, playable in your browser.

Your game files and saves stay on your device and are never uploaded.

Open the extended 2D track editor to build .cstrk tracks up to 90 × 90 tiles.

Setup instructions

You need your own Stunts (Brøderbund 1.1) game files to play. Click Choose game folder and select the folder containing those files on your device, then click Start game.

You can play directly from a website. Saving a copy of this page is optional. For offline play, save the HTML file anywhere on your device and open it in your browser. In either case, select the folder containing your game files.

Where supported, your browser remembers the folder and may ask you to allow access again next time. Otherwise, select it each time. Your game files and saves remain on your device, even when you play from a website.

Driving rules

Preparing game…

Click the game to focus the keyboard. Use the arrow keys to drive and Esc to return to menus. Show Control Layout enables the Android touch controls and starts enabled on mobile devices. Fullscreen requests landscape orientation and locks it where your browser supports it, placing phone controls in the black side areas. Select Fullscreen before starting to enter it with Start game. Your browser may reserve some function keys. Press Alt+Enter or click Fullscreen for a larger view.

Saves stay in this tab. Export them before closing, then import the saved file before starting next time. You can also import tracks and replays.
