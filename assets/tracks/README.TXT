Place your custom .TRK tracks in this folder.
SDL3 builds also support extended .CSTRK tracks.
Keep any matching .HIG or .CSHIG high-score files with the track.

Tracks are discovered automatically in the game-data folder and beside
the executable, then in their tracks subfolders. Loose tracks take
priority over tracks with the same name in this folder. Names are
matched without case sensitivity and appear once in the track list.
