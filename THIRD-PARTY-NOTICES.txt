SDL3
----
Bundled SDL3 builds include SDL's zlib license in
share/licenses/restunts/SDL-LICENSE.txt. Builds using a system SDL3 installation
use that installation's library and accompanying license instead.

DOS builds write the hardware AdLib chip and do not include Nuked OPL2 Lite.
Original game resources are supplied separately and retain their own terms.

McMurtry Speirling model reference
---------------------------------
Title: 2026 McMurtry Spéirling
Author: Dave Love SketchFab (https://sketchfab.com/Tyler_Dave)
Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/2026-mcmurtry-speirling-24a39a15bde049acbb3ede571cee0c8b
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The user-supplied GLB identifies the author, title, source and license above
in its embedded asset.extras metadata. MCMU's exterior geometry adapts that
reference into completely rebuilt low-polygon topology for the showroom and
both racing detail levels, following the supplied mesh as the exterior
reference. Changes include uniform length scaling, integer-quantized native
animated wheels, rounded and enlarged wheel areas for visible tyre clearance,
simplified lamps and rear details, native materials and seven paint schemes.
The internal filename suggests an earlier prototype despite its 2026 title.
The original high-polygon GLB and its textures are not included. The editable
adapted geometry is exported as
MCMU.obj and MCMU.mtl; runtime geometry is in STMCMU.3SH. Attribution and source
hashes are also recorded in MCMU.json and READMCMU.TXT. These changes do not
imply endorsement by the source author.
